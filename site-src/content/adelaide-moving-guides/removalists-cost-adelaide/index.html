What affects the time your move takes?

A short drive does not always mean a short move. Loading, carrying and unloading can take longer than the journey between addresses, and each factor below adds or saves minutes on site.

Choosing a crew size

Choose the crew after the inventory and access are reviewed. A 3-man crew has a higher rate than a 2-man crew, and it does not automatically mean a lower total. Ask which size suits your inventory, then confirm the rate for that crew.

How to compare moving quotes

Give each company the same information. Ask what is included, how time is charged, which call-out or travel charges apply and whether requested packing or materials add to the total. An estimated duration is not a guarantee of the final cost.

Planning tips to keep the time down

  • Pack and label the boxes you plan to pack yourself, and keep walkways clear before the crew arrives.
  • Agree on any furniture dismantling beforehand rather than leaving it for move day.
  • Book lifts and loading zones early and share the details with your removalist.
  • Move small items and loose bits into boxes so the crew can focus on furniture and heavy pieces.
  • Send photos of tight entries, stairs and large items so the time and crew size can be discussed realistically.

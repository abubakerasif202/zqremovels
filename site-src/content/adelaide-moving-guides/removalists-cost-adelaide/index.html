What affects the time your move takes?

A short drive does not always mean a short move. Loading, carrying and unloading can take longer than the journey between addresses.

  • Inventory: list large furniture, whitegoods, boxes and items from garages or outdoor areas.
  • Access: describe stairs, narrow doors, tight turns, lifts and the distance from parking to the entrance at both properties.
  • Preparation: say whether boxes are packed and furniture needs dismantling.
  • Stops: include storage collections or any additional pickup and delivery addresses.
  • Building arrangements: tell us about lift reservations, loading areas and permitted moving windows.

How to compare moving quotes

Give each company the same information. Ask what is included, how time is charged, which call-out or travel charges apply and whether requested packing or materials add to the total. An estimated duration is not a guarantee of the final cost.

Choose the crew after the inventory and access are reviewed. A larger crew has a different rate; it does not automatically mean a lower total.

Prepare before the crew arrives

Pack and label the items you plan to pack yourself, keep walkways clear and arrange building access in advance. Agree on any furniture dismantling before doing it yourself. Read the moving house checklist for preparation steps.

Information to send for a useful estimate

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, preferred date, property sizes, room-by-room inventory, approximate box count and access photos. Include stairs, lift details, parking distance, large items and any packing help required.

For local services, explore Adelaide removals. For a workplace, use office relocation planning so building access and equipment requirements are included.

Does the hourly rate include travel?

A 1-hour call-out/travel charge applies where applicable. Confirm whether it applies to your move and how it is calculated before approving the booking.

Why this checklist helps before requesting a fixed-price quote

Fixed-price moving quotes stay cleaner when the brief is specific. If ZQ Removals knows the property type, inventory size, access restrictions, parking situation, and packing requirements before quoting, the estimate is more likely to reflect the actual job instead of a rough guess.

Packing prep

Packing and fragile item preparation

Packing is where many quotes go vague. If the inventory includes glass, artwork, mirrors, monitors, lamps, or awkward furniture, call those items out early and decide whether they need professional packing or extra wrapping.

  • Label boxes by room and priority, not just contents
  • Keep documents, chargers, medication, and night-one essentials separate
  • Wrap fragile items before the crew arrives if they are self-packed
  • Leave disassembly, protection, and lift access notes in the quote brief

Apartment checklist

Apartment, lift, and loading-zone checklist

Apartment moves often turn on building access, not distance. Use this section to make sure the quote includes the real access conditions before the crew arrives.

  • Confirm lift booking times and whether the lift is passenger-only or service-enabled
  • Check parking rules, loading-zone limits, and whether a permit or booking is needed
  • Note stairs, long corridors, narrow doors, and any building manager instructions
  • Tell the team about appliances, sofa width, and oversized furniture before quoting
  • Share entry codes, concierge details, and contact numbers for the building
  • Keep building paperwork and access notes ready on move day

Move day

Final move-day checklist

The final checklist is about removing friction. If these items are covered, the move usually starts with fewer delays and less backtracking.

01

Confirm access and parking

FAQ

Questions people ask before an Adelaide move

When should I start the checklist?

Start as early as possible. Eight weeks gives enough time to collect inventory, access, packing, and timing details before you request pricing.

What detail matters most for a fixed-price quote?

The most important details are property type, inventory size, stairs or lift access, parking, and any packing or fragile-item requirements.

Does this checklist work for apartments and offices?

Yes. It is built to cover apartment lift bookings, loading zones, office timing, and the kinds of access issues that change the quote brief.

Can I call instead of filling in the form first?

Yes. Call ZQ Removals if the move is urgent or if the access is unusual. For most jobs, the checklist makes the quote form more accurate.

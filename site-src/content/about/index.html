About ZQ Removals

Founded by Qasim Ali, ZQ Removals was built on a simple principle: moving should be disciplined, transparent, and stress-free. From our base in Andrews Farm, we have grown from a single-truck operation into a trusted Adelaide removalist team serving families and businesses across South Australia and major interstate routes.

Our Mission

Why we focus on fixed pricing and careful handling.

We noticed that the biggest stress for people moving in Adelaide wasn't the heavy lifting—it was the uncertainty of hourly rates and the fear of hidden costs. ZQ Removals was created to solve that. By reviewing every move properly up front, we provide guaranteed quotes that protect our customers.

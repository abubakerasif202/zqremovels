Move Day Prep

Moving day preparation checklist

If you have booked our movers and packers, follow this quick checklist to ensure a smooth transition:

Phase 01

Separate essential items

Keep your daily essentials, keys, medications, phone chargers, and booking documentation in a separate area (like your car) so they are not packed into the truck.

Phase 02

Clear pathways and parking

Ensure driveways, stairs, corridors, and doorways are clear. Confirm elevator bookings or parking permits are active before the packers arrive.

Phase 03

Review the inventory brief

Go through the property with our crew leader to highlight any high-value items, custom handling rules, or specific priority placement needs.

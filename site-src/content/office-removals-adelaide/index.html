B2B quote preparation

Quote preparation checklist for office relocations.

A higher-value office move should include the details that affect downtime, access, IT equipment handling, archive handling, and the labelled unload plan before the quote is approved.

Downtime and timing

Confirm preferred move windows, after-hours access requirements subject to availability, staff restart priorities, and any building-management deadlines.

IT equipment and workstation sequencing

List monitors, docks, printers, peripherals, desk groups, and any equipment that should stay together for faster team reset.

Archives and document handling

Separate files, archive boxes, locked storage, and document-heavy rooms so they can be labelled and unloaded in the right area.

Building access and labelled unload

Provide lift bookings, loading dock windows, parking notes, floor plans, labels, room names, and destination placement priorities.

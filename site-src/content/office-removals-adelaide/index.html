Commercial FAQ

Questions business clients ask before approving a move.

These answers focus on access, timing, and the information needed to quote an Adelaide office move properly.

Can you move our office over a weekend to avoid disrupting business?

After-hours and weekend office moves can be assessed subject to availability. The quote should confirm building access, crew schedule, inventory, and restart priorities before the booking is accepted.

Do you handle IT equipment like computers, monitors, and printers?

Yes. We handle standard office IT equipment including desktop computers, monitors, and printers. We recommend your IT team handle server disconnection, but we transport all equipment safely.

How much does an office removal in Adelaide cost?

Office removal costs depend on the size of your premises, volume of items, number of crew required, and timing. We provide a detailed quote after reviewing the brief. Contact us to arrange a consultation.

Do you provide packing services for office moves?

Yes. Our team can pack your entire office prior to move day, including desks, storage areas, and kitchen items. Packing is quoted separately.

How far in advance should I book office removalists in Adelaide?

We recommend booking office removalists at least 4 to 6 weeks in advance. This allows sufficient time to coordinate building management approvals, reserve loading docks, sequence IT requirements, and arrange after-hours access if needed.

Do office removalists dismantle and assemble office furniture?

Yes. Our team can dismantle and reassemble standard office desks, meeting tables, and workstation partitions. Please list any custom or specialized furniture in your quote brief so we can allocate the correct tools and time.

What should a business do before office relocation day?

Before move day, you should label all workstations, pack loose items into labelled crates, confirm lift and loading dock bookings with building managers at both sites, and backup all IT systems. Our team provides an office moving checklist to help guide your prep.

Business removals by size

What to plan for, by office size.

Business removals in Adelaide vary with scale. Use the closest match as a starting point for your brief.

Small office (1–5 desks)

Label crates and list IT equipment. The main constraints are usually loading-zone parking and corridor width, so confirm packing requirements in the brief.

Medium office (10–30 desks)

Tag the floor plan and group archives. Service-lift bookings and dock clearances shape the timing, and staged unloading by department may help.

Large office or clinic

Sequence the move in stages and check stock before and after. Building-manager inductions and after-hours access should be confirmed early.

Can you move our office over a weekend to avoid disrupting business?

After-hours and weekend office moves can be assessed subject to availability. The quote should confirm building access, crew schedule, inventory, and restart priorities before the booking is accepted.

How far in advance should I book office removalists in Adelaide?

Enquire as early as you can once the date is set. Building approvals, loading-dock and lift bookings, and after-hours access often decide the lead time, and bookings are subject to crew availability.

Do office removalists dismantle and assemble office furniture?

We can dismantle and reassemble standard office desks, meeting tables, and workstation partitions. List any custom or specialised furniture in your quote brief so the right tools and time are allowed.

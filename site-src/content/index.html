Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you book.

Have a move-specific question? Call the team or include it in your quote request.

Call 0433 819 989
How much do removalists cost in Adelaide?

Cost depends on the move scope, access, distance, timing and items involved. Share those details so ZQ Removals can prepare a quote for your move.

How early should I book?

Book as early as you can once your date is known. Scheduling depends on crew, vehicle and route availability, so confirm your preferred date through the quote process.

Do you offer packing services?

Yes. Tell us which rooms or items need packing or unpacking so the work, materials and timing can be included in the move scope.

Can you move apartments and high-rise properties?

Yes. Include lift bookings, loading-zone rules, parking, stairs, corridor access and building requirements in your enquiry.

Do you disassemble and reassemble furniture?

Assembly and disassembly can be included for suitable furniture when agreed as part of the move scope. Tell us what needs attention when requesting a quote.

Do you handle interstate moves?

Yes. ZQ Removals handles interstate moves from Adelaide, with the route, inventory, access and timing reviewed before confirmation.

What happens if my moving date changes?

Contact ZQ Removals as soon as the date changes. The team will review the booking against current crew, vehicle and route availability.

Can I request an urgent or same-day move?

Yes. Urgent and same-day requests are considered subject to crew, vehicle and route availability. Call and share the move details for review.

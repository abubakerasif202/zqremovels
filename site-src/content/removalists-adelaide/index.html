Quoting Process

How ZQ Removals reviews your Adelaide moving quote

We do not believe in auto-generating an hourly estimate that changes on move day. We conduct a manual quote review to ensure there are no hidden hourly surprises.

1. Inventory & Access Checked

Your complete inventory list is reviewed before quote confirmation to ensure we allocate the right truck size and crew. We also check pickup and delivery access at both addresses before move day.

2. Stairs, Lifts & Distance

We look at the exact number of stairs, lift availability, building rules, and the walking distance between the parking spot and the door. Knowing these details upfront keeps the schedule on track.

3. Fragile & Bulky Handling

Any fragile, antique, or bulky items like marble tables, heavy wardrobes, or pianos are noted. This allows us to prepare specific protective materials and transit gear before arrival.

What a local move includes

Service scope for a local Adelaide move

A local move is a crew and a truck working through your agreed inventory at the pickup address, travelling, then unloading and placing items at the delivery address. The points below set out what that normally involves, so you can compare it against any other quote.

Crew and truck

You choose a 2-man or 3-man crew with a truck. The crew loads, drives, unloads and places items where you want them. Charging is by time, in 30-minute increments, at the published rate for the crew you select.

Protection and handling

Furniture is wrapped and secured with blankets, straps and trolleys for the carry and the drive. Heavy, fragile or awkward items should be listed in your enquiry so the crew size and time allowed suit them.

Optional extras

Packing and unpacking, and dismantling or reassembling suitable furniture, can be added when agreed as part of the move. Mention them early because they add time on site.

How to enquire

How to send an Adelaide moving enquiry

The clearer the details, the easier it is to say which crew size and how much time suit your move. The final time can vary, so confirm the crew rate and any call-out/travel charge before booking.

1. Send the essentials

Include the pickup and delivery suburbs, your preferred date, the property type and a room-by-room list of what is moving. A rough box count helps too.

2. Add access details

Note the number of stairs, lift availability, building rules and the walking distance from the parking spot to the door at both addresses. Photos of tight entries are useful.

3. Flag fragile and heavy items

Marble tables, large wardrobes, pianos and other heavy or fragile pieces should be named in the enquiry, so the handling plan and time allowed reflect them.

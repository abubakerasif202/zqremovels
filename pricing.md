# Pricing — ZQ Removals

ZQ Removals operates on a **Fixed-Price Quote** model. We do not use hidden hourly rates. Every proposal is built around your specific move brief to ensure budget certainty.

## Pricing Logic & Variables
Our quotes are calculated based on the following key operational factors:

### 1. Property Type & Volume
- **Small Apartment/Unit:** 1-2 bedrooms, typically requires smaller trucks and focused crews.
- **Family Home:** 3-5+ bedrooms, requires larger capacity and multi-person teams.
- **Office/Commercial:** Scoped by workstation count, equipment types, and restart timing.

### 2. Access Conditions
- **Stairs:** Number of flights and tightness of landings.
- **Lifts:** Service lift availability and booking windows.
- **Parking:** Proximity of loading zones or driveway constraints.
- **Long Carries:** Distance from the property entrance to the truck.

### 3. Route & Logistics
- **Local Moves:** Within the Adelaide metropolitan area.
- **Interstate Moves:** Long-haul routes (e.g., Adelaide to Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Perth).
- **Storage Stops:** If the move requires a transit or long-term storage handover.

### 4. Additional Services
- **Professional Packing:** Full or partial home packing using premium materials.
- **Fragile Handling:** Specialist protection for delicate furniture, art, or IT equipment.
- **After-Hours/Weekend:** Premium slots for moves requiring specific timing windows.

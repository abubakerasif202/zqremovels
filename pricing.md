# Pricing — ZQ Removals

ZQ Removals provides professional fixed-price moving quotes for Adelaide and South Australia. We do not use "open-ended" hourly rates that can lead to unexpected costs. Every move is quoted based on a specific inventory and access brief.

## Pricing Model: Guaranteed Fixed Price
- **Quote Basis:** Inventory list + Property access (stairs/lifts/parking) + Distance.
- **Inclusions:** Professional crew, truck, furniture blankets, transit insurance, and fuel.
- **No Hidden Fees:** The price quoted is the final price for the agreed scope.

## Service Benchmarks (Estimated Ranges)
While every quote is custom, the following ranges represent typical professional standards for Adelaide removals (including GST):

### Small Moves (Apartments / Small Homes)
- **Typical Setup:** 2 Men + Small-Medium Truck
- **Estimated Value Range:** $450 - $850+ (subject to inventory and access)
- **Best For:** 1-2 bedroom apartments, small unit relocations.

### Medium Moves (Standard 3-4 Bedroom Homes)
- **Typical Setup:** 2-3 Men + Large Truck
- **Estimated Value Range:** $900 - $1,600+ (subject to inventory and access)
- **Best For:** Suburban family homes, large office suites.

### Large or Complex Moves
- **Typical Setup:** 3-4 Men + Large Truck(s)
- **Estimated Value Range:** Custom Quote Required
- **Best For:** 5+ bedroom homes, multi-story office relocations.

## Additional Services
- **Professional Packing:** Custom quote based on box count and fragility.
- **Interstate Removals:** Fixed-price quotes for Adelaide to Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Perth, and Canberra.
- **Urgent / Same-Day:** Subject to availability; priority booking may apply.

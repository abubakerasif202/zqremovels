# ZQ Video Ad Prompting

## Overview
ZQ Removals is a premium, high-precision removalist brand in Adelaide. Video ads must reject generic stock-move tropes (stressed homeowners, generic acoustic-pop music) and instead project editorial authority, operational expertise, and absolute security.

## Core Script Framework
Every video ad script must follow the **Hook-Pain-Proof-CTA** framework, tailored to ZQ's unique positioning:

1. **The Hook (0-3s)**: Visually striking, high-contrast, text-led, disrupting the scroll. Show real Adelaide landmarks or high-end furniture handling.
2. **The Pain (3-10s)**: Target "Hourly-Rate Stuffing" (movers taking slow routes, taking breaks on your dime) or "Apartment Logistical Lockout" (building managers rejecting moves due to lift/gate issues).
3. **The Proof (10-22s)**: Highlight ZQ's fixed-price guarantee (no hidden fees) and precision access planning (lift bookings, loading zones, stair carry).
4. **The CTA (22-30s)**: Direct driving action to get a "Fixed-Price Move Brief" at `zqremovals.au`.

## Aesthetic Directives
- **Visuals**: Modern, cinematic, editorial. Dark forest greens, gold accents, crisp lighting, high-end residential interiors.
- **Audio**: Sound design-heavy (tactile impacts, ambient low hums, crisp tape slices, smooth jazz or elegant lo-fi beats).
- **Text**: On-screen typography using elegant serif or clean geometric sans-serif (no generic comic/basic fonts).

## Reference Video Prompt Template
Use the following prompt when instructing a copywriter agent:
"""
You are a cinematic copywriter. Write a 30-second vertical video ad script for ZQ Removals.
Target: Adelaide professionals & apartment dwellers moving home.
Tone: Premium, assured, precise, low-stress.
Requirements:
1. Hook: Start with a text overlay: "They get paid hourly. You pay for the traffic." or similar hook targeting rate-stuffing.
2. Visuals: Describe cinematic, high-contrast scenes (leafy Adelaide suburbs, modern apartments, gold-and-forest branding).
3. Action: Highlight ZQ's 1-minute fixed-price move brief process.
4. Format: Audio/Visual split table.
"""

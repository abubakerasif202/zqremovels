## Phase 2: Real local backlinks (weeks 2-8)

These earn links that move rankings. Each needs a genuine relationship or genuinely useful content.

1. **Industry body.** Check eligibility for a moving-industry association such as the Australian Furniture Removers Association. Only apply if the business actually qualifies.
2. **Local business bodies.** Business SA, local chamber of commerce, and the City of Playford business directory (Andrews Farm area). Check what each offers.
3. **Referral partners (strongest in this niche).** Real estate agents and property managers, end-of-lease cleaners, storage companies, conveyancers, and apartment strata managers. Offer a "moving partner" mention in exchange for a "recommended removalist" link on their page.
4. **Sponsorships.** Local sports clubs and community events usually include a sponsor link.
5. **Linkable assets.** Pages worth citing, already partly on the site: the published-rate page (`/removalists-adelaide-prices/`), moving checklists, apartment/CBD access guides. Pitch these to Adelaide property blogs, uni housing pages and relocation guides.
6. **Local press.** A short story on a real, local angle (for example a community move or a charity job). Only pitch what actually happened.

## What not to do

- No bought links, PBNs, link exchanges at scale or comment spam. They risk a manual penalty.
- No fake or incentivised reviews, and no review gating. The site already has about 100 real Google reviews; keep asking happy customers.
- No identical copy across listings; vary the descriptions.

## Reviews and local signals

- After each job, send customers the Google review link. Ask them to mention the suburb and the type of move (for example "apartment move in Glenelg").
- Reply to every review within a few days, including critical ones.
- Add new profile URLs to `sameAs` in the site's schema once they are live. This is a small repo change I can make.

2. Bing Places for Business.
3. Apple Business Connect.
4. Facebook page: match name, phone, website and description.
5. Yellow Pages Australia, True Local, Hotfrog Australia, Localsearch / StartLocal.
6. Oneflare and ServiceSeeking (also check their fees and lead terms first).

For each: use section 1 exactly, a different description from section 2, real photos only, and record the final listing URL in a sheet so it can go into the site's `sameAs` schema.

## 6. Rules to keep

- Send only to people you have a real reason to contact. No bulk or purchased lists.
- Do not offer money or free moves in exchange for links or reviews.
- Do not ask customers for only five-star reviews, and do not write reviews for them.
- Track every contact in a sheet: date, who, outcome, and the live link if won.

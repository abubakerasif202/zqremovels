### Existing versus added coverage

Broad removalist, near-me, affordable, pricing and specialist coverage already existed. This change strengthens ownership and customer utility rather than claiming entirely new keyword reach. Added prominence: moving-company language on home; local movers and suburb-to-suburb wording on the local hub; affordable/budget wording and total-cost comparison on cheap page. Exact permutations such as “we are removals near me” were deliberately not inserted. Furniture near-me intent belongs to the actual furniture service with local-hub support.

Near-me searches require location relevance and route suitability, not repeated phrases. Affordable searches need cost-control advice and inclusions. Pricing searches need actual rates and billing terms. Specialised searches need access and inventory information for that service. Broad searches need a clear overview and a useful next step.
